# Future reconstruction research

Architecture direction only: the MVP does not implement photogrammetry.

With institutional authorization and rights review, future work may examine multi-view collection imagery, SfM/MVS, camera pose estimation, silhouette comparison, surface correspondence, multiple-hypothesis comparison, expert review, and IIIF 3D integration.

- AI-generated missing views are speculative aids, never evidence.
- One image cannot be presented as definite 3D fact.
- Every input requires rights review.
- Public outputs remain non-manufacturing visual proxies.
- Uncertainty and rejected alternatives remain visible.
- `PhotogrammetryResearchBackend` stays unimplemented until a separately reviewed protocol exists.
