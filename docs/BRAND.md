## Voice and appearance

Objective, restrained, academic, transparent, bilingual, legible. The visual system uses museum-paper neutrals, archival ink, muted ochre, moss, editorial serif display typography, compact evidence labels, and deliberately calm motion.

Never present the project as a store, movement tutorial, evaluation channel, trading platform, manufacturing guide, or danger-oriented short-video product. Avoid sensational, sales, superiority, tactical, operation-guide, and challenge language.
