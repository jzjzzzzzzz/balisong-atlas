# Open media review

Review date: 2026-09-01

This register records the public visual-media pass used by the browser kinetic
study. It is separate from the 97-record literature corpus. Files were selected
manually from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded through an allow-listed script, and
hashed in `data/research/media-register.json`.

## Selection result

| Group | Records | Public use | Research role |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Continuous motion | 3 | Complete loop, source and license visible | Broad body order, orientation, transition continuity |
| Matched open/closed photos | 2 | Display with attribution | Contemporary visible-state comparison |
| Appearance photos | 2 | Display with bounded observation note | Surface, color, broad silhouette |
| Museum context | 1 | Context label only | Display and photographic context |
| Uploader-dated lead | 1 | “Dating unverified” label | Provenance research lead only |

## Source-family decision

The two 2011 DJLO GIFs share a creator and visual setting. They are one source
family and cannot be counted as independent agreement. The 2020 Gumballwolf GIF
is a second family. Both are modern self-published records (tier D), so neither
supports a historical-performance claim.

The Ringer open/closed photographs also form one matched source family. Their
pairing is useful for visible-state comparison, not for additional independent
confidence.

## Public-media controls

- no frame step, slow motion, speed display, angle overlay, or motion path;
- no grip, hand-position, action-step, or technique annotation;
- no measurement, perspective calibration, or scale inference;
- no claim extraction, embeddings, or corpus QA from motion-media content;
- modern motion never dates a historical period;
- uploader descriptions remain untrusted until corroborated;
- every local file is tied to a source page, Creative Commons license, and SHA-256.

## 3D translation decision

Motion track version 2 uses the records only to improve continuity. It adds
smooth whole-object pitch/yaw/roll and small exhibition-space translation while
keeping the same abstract three-body relationship. Its loop cadence is synthetic
and is not copied from any source. Period appearance presets continue to use
their document/image evidence independently of the motion track.
