## Sequence and decisions

### 2026-08-24 — existing corpus audit

Inspected the existing 97-record Balisong Atlas bibliography, 81 content-addressed research downloads, screening report, and nine-item public media register. Confirmed that many early scans had already been acquired lawfully from university or government repositories. Separated “found,” “downloaded,” and “read”: only the last category can support a strong claim. Identified controlled passages in patents, reference chapters, and periodicals; retained only the fact that controlled technical or operational content was excluded.

### 2026-08-25 — Philippine institutional search

Searched De La Salle University, University of the Philippines repositories, University of Batangas, the Municipality of Taal, the Cultural Center of the Philippines, the National Library of the Philippines, Philippine eLib, IPOPHL, and Lawphil. Re-opened the DLSU Galvan record and confirmed that only an abstract is publicly available. Re-opened the UPLB Cabanding record and likewise classified it as abstract-only. Recorded the University of Batangas Likhalina page as contemporary institutional framing rather than evidence of antiquity. Treated the Taal municipal pages as local self-representation with a promotional bias. Checked IPOPHL’s general geographical-indications page and found no basis for claiming that balisong is registered as a Philippine GI.

### 2026-08-26 — dated Philippine texts

Read the 1951 Lardizabal report and verified the Batangas industry reference on printed page 18. Read the 12 October 1947 *Ilang-Ilang* issue and verified that “balisong” appears as intelligible popular vocabulary; classified the fiction as evidence of term circulation only. Read the CCP *Metalcraft* extract, especially PDF folio 5, and recorded its Batangas/Taal institutional framing while excluding its technical passages. Read Yasa’s UPOU thesis at pages 75 and 97 and verified its citation chain to a 1969 Nayong Pilipino brochure. The original brochure remains unavailable locally, so the later thesis cannot become a substitute primary text.

### 2026-08-27 — material culture and heritage theory

Verified publisher metadata for Appadurai and Kopytoff and limited their use to the available preview and stable chapter information. Downloaded and read Star and Griesemer’s boundary-object article and Gosden and Marshall’s object-biography article. Checked Dyer’s open-access state-of-field article and Smith’s open heritage-discourse article. Checked UNESCO’s official pages on intangible heritage and traditional craftsmanship. The paper therefore treats “heritage” as a present social and institutional practice, not an automatic synonym for age.

### 2026-08-28 — affordance and interaction aesthetics

Downloaded and read Hekkert, Ross and Wensveen, van Dijk and Rietveld, and Ramstead, Veissière, and Kirmayer. Checked Rietveld and Kiverstein’s full open article. These sources required a revision of the working thesis: paired handles are described as an enabling affordance within sociomaterial practice, not as a sufficient mechanical cause. No real dimensions, construction details, or inferred internal structure were recorded.

### 2026-08-29 — participation and media

Downloaded and read Jenkins et al.’s MacArthur paper and Farnsworth, Kleanthous, and Wenger-Trayner’s accepted manuscript; checked Cox’s repository article. The search found no comparable peer-reviewed ethnography of present balisong performance communities. The paper therefore does not claim continuity between local craft transmission and later online performance. It describes a possible reformatting under participatory media and identifies the missing ethnography as a research gap. Existing motion files were not used or embedded because they contain operational sequences.

### 2026-08-30 — legal texts

Checked 15 U.S.C. § 1241 against the U.S. House code edition showing laws in effect on 29 August 2026. Read the official 21 May 1971 Federal Register notice at 36 Fed. Reg. 9263, California Attorney General Opinion 85-902, the DOJ’s July 1988 bulletin, and full opinions in *Taylor*, *Riddall*, *Strange*, *Quattrone*, and *Lasanas*. Recorded majority/dissent and procedural posture separately. Excluded mechanism descriptions and all user-facing legal advice. Did not include a jurisdiction table.

### 2026-08-31 — matrices before prose

Completed a 50-source candidate matrix and download manifest. Created the claim–evidence matrix before drafting. Rejected simple-origin, uninterrupted-continuity, UNESCO-designation, GI-registration, and national-culture generalizations. Marked the paper’s cross-domain synthesis as interpretation, not a fact directly stated by any source.

### 2026-09-01 — verification and publication preparation

Reopened cited locators, checked DOI strings, regenerated SHA-256 values for fifteen lawful local research copies, and prepared a rights manifest. Image selection was intentionally reduced to public-domain or licensed contextual figures plus original diagrams. The legal as-of date remains 2026-09-01. The article remains a Research Draft and has not been peer reviewed.
