# Excluded sources

Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

The following source families are excluded from evidentiary use: product listings; commercial brand histories; collector and discussion forums; Reddit and social media posts; action-instruction sites and videos; anonymous blogs; AI-generated pages; SEO histories that repeat an origin story without provenance; Wikipedia; pirated copies; paywalled texts not lawfully accessed; patents or manuals whose primary evidentiary content is technical. Wikipedia was used only to discover search terms and is absent from the bibliography.

Specific exclusions are also recorded in `source_matrix.csv`. When an otherwise useful source contains exact measurements, manufacturing or assembly descriptions, operational sequences, purchasing information, or legal evasion guidance, the research record stores only the flag **contains excluded technical content**. It does not reproduce the information.
