# Legal source verification

**As-of date: 2026-09-01**

This paper analyzes legal classification historically and conceptually. It does not provide legal advice concerning possession, purchase, transport, or use.

Verification rules:

1. Federal statutory language was checked against the U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel edition showing laws in effect on 2026-08-29.
2. The 1971 Customs notice was checked against the official Federal Register scan on GovInfo.
3. Judicial holdings were checked against full opinions or dependable reproductions of the reported decisions; procedural outcome and dissent were kept distinct.
4. California Attorney General Opinion 85-902 was checked against the Attorney General's official PDF.
5. Philippine legal use was checked against the Supreme Court E-Library/Lawphil text of *People v. Lasanas*; it is used only as evidence of legal vocabulary and historical classification context, not as an origin or design case.
6. The legal section is not a current jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction guide. It omits possession, purchase, transport, and use instructions.

No claim is made that this draft exhausts later amendments, local ordinances, unpublished decisions, or all procedural history. Any future publication must repeat the status check.
